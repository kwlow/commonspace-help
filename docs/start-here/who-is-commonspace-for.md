# Who Is Commonspace For?

Not sure if Commonspace is the right fit? Here's how different types of owners use it. If you haven't read [What Is Commonspace?](/docs/start-here/what-is-commonspace) yet, that's the better starting point.

---

## Creators

You've built an audience on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, or a newsletter — and you want a home base that's actually yours.

- Offer subscription Memberships with exclusive content
- Publish posts directly to paying members
- Let fans support you without a platform taking a cut of the relationship

## Musicians & Artists

Fan communities built on streaming platforms or social media are fragile — they exist at the platform's discretion. On Commonspace, you own the fan relationship directly.

- Offer member tiers with real-world Benefits like merchandise redemptions or backstage access
- Publish content that goes straight to your most dedicated fans

## Brands

Loyalty programs built on third-party platforms give you engagement but not ownership. Commonspace gives you a fully branded community — your domain, your colors, your logo.

- Direct access to your members' emails and activity
- Offer Memberships and other digital products with Benefits attached to purchases
- Keep every member relationship in a place you control

## Professional Organizations

Industry networks, associations, and membership organizations often stitch together a newsletter tool, a forum, and a paywall — and none of it talks to each other. Commonspace gives you all of it in one place.

- Paid Memberships with members-only content
- Private Channels for discussion
- Full ownership of your member data

## Conferences & Events

An event's community shouldn't disappear when the event ends. Use Commonspace to build a Space before your event, keep it active during, and maintain it afterward as an ongoing community.

- Offer member Benefits like exclusive content and downloadable materials
- Redeemable perks for attendees
- Publish posts to keep the conversation going year-round
